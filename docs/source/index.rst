Zipf's Law
===================================

Welcome to the documentation for the Zipf's Law project. This project is a test of the Zipf's Law, which is an empirical law that states that the frequency of a word is inversely proportional to its rank. This means that the most frequent word will appear twice as often as the second most frequent word, three times as often as the third most frequent word, and so on. The Zipf's Law has been observed in many natural languages, as well as in other areas such as the distribution of city sizes and the popularity of websites.
